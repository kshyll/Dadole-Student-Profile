Who I am

About me

I am a BS Information Technology student at Xavier University, focused on mobile application development, web development, and UI/UX design. In my free time, I explore Cybersecurity and sometimes Embedded Systems.

Outside of academics, I am involved in student leadership and actively participate in several organizations, including the Council of Computer Studies, Xavier Circle of Information Technology, GDG-CDO, RoboGals-CDO Chapter, and WTM-CDO.

My goal is to build a fruitful, stable, and dedicated career as a developer while creating applications that are useful and essential to the community. I aim to contribute to society not only through my career but also through the impact of my work, continuously paying it forward and using my skills to help others.

Outside of tech, I enjoy yoga, reading, and hiking, which help me stay grounded and balanced.
